FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Do I own my accounts?

Yes — 100%. Your Google, Facebook and Instagram accounts are registered under your business name and information. We work as your manager; ownership never transfers to us.

Do you guarantee a 5-star rating?

No — and nobody honest can. Ratings come from real customers. What we guarantee is the work: every review answered within one business day, your profiles active every week, and a clear report every month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Service is month-to-month; cancel anytime with 30 days' written notice (an email is fine). We hand over all credentials and content and remove our access within 7 days.

Who writes the posts?

Our team does — from your menu, your photos, and your specials. You approve the style at the start, and you can ask for changes to anything we publish at any time.

What do you need from me to start?

Just the basics, once: your business information (hours, menu, services), a few photos, and a one-time setup step — granting us manager access and passing along any verification code sent to your phone. After that, we run it.
